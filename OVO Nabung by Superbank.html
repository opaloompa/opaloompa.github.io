OVO Nabung by Superbank

Connecting everyday payments with high-yield savings through a joined-up OVO and Superbank experience.

Role UX Designer
Timeline Q4 2024
Team / research 3 UX Designers · 1 UX Writer · 3 Project Managers

The opportunity

OVO has a large base of people using its e-wallet for frequent transactions, while Superbank was a new entrant with low user acquisition. The initiative brought a savings product into the OVO ecosystem, offering 5% p.a. interest and a way to turn everyday payment activity into saving.

The challenge

Users kept money available for daily purchases, but e-wallet balances did not earn interest. Moving savings to a separate banking app added KYC, app switching, and manual transfers. Users also found financial-yield language complex and could drop off during cross-app handoffs.

My contribution

I owned the in-app Superbank experience: the savings dashboard, Saku setup and management, payment execution, QRIS and transfer flows, and transaction history. I collaborated with the OVO team on microcopy, shared design tokens, error states, and deep-link behavior.

Designing connected journeys

We shaped smooth handoffs from OVO onboarding back into Superbank, consistent financial terminology across both apps, and a modular Saku experience that keeps savings organized while connected to ecosystem payments. Payment flows clarify funding sources and give users balance feedback after a transaction.

Reported outcomes

The project brief reports more than 1 million registered users after 60 days, with 60% coming from the OVO Nabung integration. It also reports 2.3 million+ total users, 3× growth in offline merchant transactions, and more than doubled usage across GrabFood, GrabBike, and GrabCar.

+1M registered users after 60 days
60% users from OVO integration
2.3M+ total users reported

Reflection

Ecosystem UX depends on clear handoffs across products. Post-launch feedback still showed some users had questions about how interest and balances worked across OVO and Superbank, and about the value of linking accounts.
