\## Full Introduction

## Short Introduction

I'm Naufal Rifqi, a Product Designer with 4+ years of experience shaping digital products across fintech, NGOs, and consumer applications. I turn complex problems into intuitive, accessible, and meaningful user experiences.

Currently, I'm working at Superbank Indonesia, where I focus on designing digital banking experiences and building scalable design systems.

## My Design Philosophy

I believe good design starts with understanding people, not jumping straight into conclusions. I approach product design by connecting user needs, business goals, and technical feasibility to create experiences that are useful, clear, and sustainable.

My process combines research, structured problem-solving, thoughtful interface design, and close collaboration with engineers and stakeholders.

## What I Bring to the Table

### Research & Strategy

I translate user needs and product challenges into actionable insights through research, usability testing, and journey mapping.

### Product & UI Design

I design intuitive interfaces and end-to-end product experiences, balancing usability, visual clarity, and business requirements.

### Design Systems

I build reusable components, consistent design patterns, and documentation that help teams scale their products efficiently.

### Cross-functional Collaboration

I work closely with product managers, engineers, and stakeholders to turn ideas into feasible solutions and ensure design intent carries through implementation.

## Beyond the Screen

Outside of product design, I'm interested in running, visual storytelling, and exploring how design can create meaningful experiences beyond digital products.

## Personal Note

I'm always curious about how things work, why people behave the way they do, and how small improvements can make everyday experiences better.
