BTN Tunas: Flexible Loan Service for MSME

A concept proposal for flexible financial assistance that supports business growth for Indonesian MSMEs.

Role Concept proposal
Timeline Not specified
Team / research Qualitative interviews · 5 users

Context

BTN (Bank Tabungan Negara) is a state-owned bank providing funding to individual and business customers. The proposal explored a platform for micro, small, and medium-sized businesses to access flexible financial assistance.

Disclaimer: This project was created strictly as a concept proposal.

User need

Business owners may need loans to grow, but collateral requirements and in-person applications can be difficult to manage alongside daily operations.

Opportunity

How might we offer financial services and loans that are easy to use, avoid burdensome requirements, and adapt to each user’s business performance?

Proposed direction

The concept explored a loan-channeling app with flexible options and insights to help businesses grow. Ideas were narrowed around the problem statement and prioritized against the proposed direction.

Core flow

The experience centers on onboarding, accessing a loan limit, and repayment. The proposed onboarding moves through phone verification, account checks or ID capture, personal and business details, identity verification, and account opening. Repayment includes flexible and full repayment options.

Research and testing

The proposal was tested through qualitative interviews with five financial-app users aged 18–35, including financially savvy participants, entrepreneurs, and digital nomads.

3 of 5 said onboarding felt long
4 of 5 wanted clarity on business data use
5 of 5 wanted disbursement split into steps

Testing insights

Testing found that users wanted clarity about why business details were collected, and preferred disbursement broken into steps. Five of five participants could find their latest transaction and understood where flexible repayment was available. Three of five understood that a home-screen payment covered only the installment due for that period.

Lessons learned

Data can help teams make stakeholder decisions and clarify business impact. Too many repayment choices can create confusion, so a limited set of guided flexible options may be easier to use.
