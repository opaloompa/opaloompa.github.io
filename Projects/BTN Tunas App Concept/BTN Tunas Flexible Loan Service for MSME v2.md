# Overview

BTN (Bank Tabungan Negara) is a state-owned bank that focuses on providing funding for both individual and business customers. In its efforts to reach entrepreneurs across various regions in Indonesia, 

<aside>
💡 Disclaimer: this project was made strictly as a concept proposal

</aside>

**BTN is taking the initiative to offer a platform for micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) to access flexible financial assistance that benefits their business growth.**

## Understanding Business Goal

> **Improve the onboarding process** by eliminating unnecessary steps to boost user conversion rates. **Provide access to flexible financial solutions** and enhance the efficiency of fund distribution.
> 

## Understanding The Users

<img src="/Projects/BTN Tunas App Concept/BTN-Tunas_Insights.png" />

<aside>

💡 **Insight**

A lot of **users are eager and willing** to get loans to help grow their businesses. But **many loan providers, especially banks, require collateral** if users want to apply for business purposes. On top of that, **the application process often requires users to visit physical offices**, which is a hassle given their operational activities.

**How might we** offer financial services (loans) that are not only easy to use but also don’t come with burdensome requirements and can be tailored to each user’s business performance?

</aside>

### Testing Result

On the testing phase, the approach that I used is a qualitative methods by doing user interview to 5 different user with various demographic backgrounds, with specific criteria regarding their experience using financial app. 

- **Age:** 18-35 Years old
- **Gender:** Male/Female
- **Behaviour:** Financial savvy, Entrepreneur, Digital nomad

<img src="/Projects/BTN Tunas App Concept/BTN-Tunas_testing-1.png" />

<img src="/Projects/BTN Tunas App Concept/BTN-Tunas_testing-2.png" />

<img src="/Projects/BTN Tunas App Concept/BTN-Tunas_testing-3.png" />

From the interview, I can synthesize that:

- **During the onboarding** proccess, **3 of 5** users are concern about the steps that taking too much time to fill but also aware as this is common in banking and financial apps
- **2 of 5** users are not willing to fill out more than 1 emergency contact during the onboarding
- **4 of 5** users are willing to fill out the business detail, but confused on what will bank do with their business data
- **3 of 5** users can easily navigate the dashboard and find the disburse button
- **5 of 5** users hoping to see disbursement in some steps to reduce their confusion on filling out the details
- **3 of 5** users are aware that paying bills from homescreen entry point will only paid the installment that due on certain period
- **5 of 5** users can find out their latest transaction easily
- **5 of 5** users aware they can do flexible repayment from the loan detail page

# Lesson Learned 💡

From this project I learn a lot on how to manage stakeholder decision with a data driven solution in order to convince and give clear business direction that will be impactful. Also that flexible financial servicing products for MSME user can help them grow and convince them to seek help from the financial services provider. 

Another key takeaway from this project is to limit the flexibility option to user as it’s tend to give more complexity and confusion to the user. Giving both flexible option and guided action to the user might reduce the complexity yet still manage to offer the solution.
