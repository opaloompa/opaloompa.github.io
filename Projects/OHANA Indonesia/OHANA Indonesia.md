Ohana Indonesia is a non-governmental organization dedicated to advocating for disability rights in Indonesia. As a long-time client, they sought a fresh, modern redesign for their website to improve accessibility, engagement, and user experience. The goal was to create a visually compelling platform that effectively communicates their mission and programs to a broader audience.

# Findings

- **Unclear focus:** The website lacks a clear focus on its programs, donation opportunities, or affiliate opportunities.
- **Confusing navigation:** Some pages link to incorrect destinations, and the many submenus can be confusing.
- **No SEO or analytics integration**

# The Challenge

Since this is my first time working with a nonprofit focused on disability rights, I need to pay close attention to WCAG guidelines to make the platform accessible to users with a range of needs, while maintaining a clear sitemap hierarchy and intuitive functionality.

> **HMW** design an inclusive platform that meets full WCAG compliance without compromising on a modern, visually engaging user experience?

<img src="/Projects/OHANA Indonesia/OHANA Indonesia Portfolio.png" />

# Approach

- **Modern & Accessible Design:** A responsive layout with high-contrast elements for better readability.
- **Streamlined Navigation:** Simplified menu structure to ensure easy access to key information.
- **Engaging Visuals:** A balanced combination of imagery, typography, and whitespace for an inviting user experience.
- **Multilingual Support:** Optimized for both Indonesian and English audiences.
- **Enhanced Content Presentation:** Structured sections to highlight advocacy efforts, programs, and impact stories effectively.
