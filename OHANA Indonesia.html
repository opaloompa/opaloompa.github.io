OHANA Indonesia

A clearer, more accessible website for an organization advocating for disability rights in Indonesia.

RoleWeb Designer
Timeline2025
Team1 Web Designer · 1 Product Owner

Making the mission easier to find

OHANA Indonesia is a nonprofit organization advocating for disability rights. The redesign aimed to improve accessibility, engagement, and the experience of sharing its mission and programs with a broader audience.

What needed to change

  • The site did not clearly surface programs, donation opportunities, or ways to get involved.
  • Deep menus and incorrect destinations made navigation confusing.
  • SEO and analytics were not integrated.

The design challenge was to build an inclusive experience with a clear sitemap and accessible patterns while keeping the site modern and engaging.

A more accessible, focused experience

The approach used responsive layouts, high contrast, simplified navigation, and clearer content structure. The site also supports both Indonesian and English audiences.

Reported results

The project notes an 11% returning-customer figure after launch, organic visitors arriving through Google Search, and an average of 1.9 pages per visit.
