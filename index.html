A little about me

01 / Approach

I’m a product designer with 4+ years of experience shaping digital products across fintech, NGOs, and consumer applications. Currently, I work at Superbank Indonesia on digital banking experiences and scalable design systems.

Good design starts with understanding people. I connect user needs, business goals, and technical feasibility through research, structured problem-solving, thoughtful interface design, and close collaboration.

Outside work, I’m interested in running, visual storytelling, and how design can make everyday experiences more meaningful.
