FintechUI/UXMobile App

BTN Tunas: Flexible Loan Service for MSME

Redesigning a micro-lending flow to help small business owners access capital without friction — from application to disbursement.

Role
Lead UI/UX Designer
Year
2024
Duration
4 months
Tools
Figma · Maze · FigJam
BTN

Overview

Bank Tabungan Negara (BTN) wanted to digitize their MSME loan product — BTN Tunas — targeting small business owners who previously had to visit a branch to apply. The challenge was designing a mobile flow that felt trustworthy, simple enough for first-time digital users, and fast enough to reduce abandonment.

"How do we make a loan application feel less like a form, and more like a conversation?"

Impact

62%
Drop-off Reduced
4.6★
Usability Score
3 min
Avg. Apply Time

Process

01

Discovery & Research

Conducted 12 contextual interviews with MSME owners across Jakarta and Surabaya. Identified trust signals and progress clarity as the biggest drop-off drivers.

02

Information Architecture

Restructured the 18-step legacy flow into a 5-phase wizard. Used card sorting to validate category groupings with real users before wireframing.

03

UI Design & Prototyping

Designed a high-fidelity prototype in Figma with accessible color system, large tap targets, and clear status feedback at each step.

04

Testing & Iteration

3 rounds of usability testing via Maze. Iterated on document upload UX and loan simulator design based on session recordings.

NGOWeb DesignAccessibility

Accessible Website to Promote Disability Rights Awareness with Ohana Indonesia

A fresh, inclusive redesign for Indonesia's leading disability rights NGO — built for everyone, including the people they advocate for.

Role
UI/UX & Web Designer
Year
2024
Duration
3 months
Tools
Figma · Framer · Lighthouse
OI

Overview

Ohana Indonesia is a non-governmental organization dedicated to advocating for disability rights. As a long-time client, they needed a modern redesign that reflected their mission while dramatically improving accessibility — for their staff, their community, and the people they serve.

Accessibility isn't a feature — it's a foundation. Every design decision was evaluated through the lens of WCAG 2.1 AA compliance and real user impact.

Impact

AA
WCAG Compliant
94
Lighthouse Score
2.4×
Session Duration

Process

01

Accessibility Audit

Ran a full WCAG audit of the existing site. Found 47 critical issues including contrast failures, missing alt text, and keyboard-inaccessible navigation.

02

Co-design with Users

Worked with 6 participants across visual, motor, and cognitive disability groups to co-design navigation patterns and content hierarchy.

03

Design & Build

Created an accessible design system in Figma — high-contrast tokens, scalable type, focus states — then implemented it in Framer with semantic HTML.

Superbank Design System

A unified component library powering Superbank's Funding, Lending, and core banking products — from design tokens to shipped UI.

Role
Product Designer

SB

Overview

Superbank is a digital-first bank in Indonesia. When I joined, the product team was shipping inconsistent UI across features, slowing down development and creating a fragmented user experience. My mission: build a scalable design system that bridges design and engineering.

A design system is never "done" — it's a living product. My job is to make every designer and engineer's job easier, every sprint.

What I Built

🎨

Token Architecture

Designed a 3-tier token system (primitive → semantic → component) supporting light/dark mode and brand theming across all Superbank products.

🧩

Component Library

200+ components in Figma — fully auto-layout, variant-structured, and documented. Connected to Storybook for engineering handoff.

📖

Documentation

Wrote usage guidelines, do/don't examples, and accessibility notes for every component. Published on Zeroheight for cross-team access.
